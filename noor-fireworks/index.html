Six rules for a happy night.

  1. Read the label and the distance printed on every item.
  2. Light at arm's length, then walk back straight away.
  3. Fire on flat, firm ground, away from wires and trees.
  4. Never return to an item that did not go off.
  5. Keep a bucket of water or sand nearby.
  6. An adult lights every firework. Children watch.

Questions before you order

Which cities do you deliver to?

Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar and Hyderabad. For other cities, message us and we will check the courier route.

How do I pay?

Send your order on WhatsApp and we reply with the total and delivery charge. Pay half by Easypaisa, JazzCash or bank transfer, and the rest when your order arrives.

How does buy one, get one free work?

It covers our 16-shot and 25-shot cakes. Add any two and the cheaper one is free. Add four and the two cheaper ones are free. Your cart shows the saving before you send the order.

Can I use fireworks inside a hall or marquee?

Only cold spark (ice) fountains. They have no flame or hot tip and are rated for indoor use. Everything else is for outdoors only.

What does "Keep back" mean on a product?

It is the minimum distance your guests should stand from the firework once it is lit. Measure it before the night, not during.

What if it rains on our wedding night?

Light rain is fine; our gear is weather-sealed. In heavy rain or strong wind we move the display to the next free date at no extra charge.

Do I need a licence to buy big fireworks?

Yes. Display shells marked "Licensed only" are sold to licensed operators. Send us your licence details and we will share trade prices.
