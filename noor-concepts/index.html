Three ideas for Noor

Each idea is built around one thing a visitor can do that no fireworks shop online lets them do. Same Noor colours and type as today; the difference is the experience. Open each one, try it, and tell us which to build into the full site.

Idea 1

Build your show

The shop is a Karachi rooftop. Pick fireworks, they line up on the roof, press Fire and watch your own show. The show you built is your order.

Learns from playful, game-like sites such as Messenger by abeto, Awwwards Developer Site of the Year 2025.

Idea 2

One wedding night

Scroll moves a clock from 7 pm to 11:30 pm. Baraat, mehndi, dinner, rukhsati: each moment fires the products used then, with sound you can switch on.

Learns from scroll storytelling sites such as Santioni Spirits (FWA of the Month) and Shopify Editions.

Idea 3

Names in the sky

Type the couple's names and rockets spell them across the sky. Drag to draw with fire. It leads to a real product: a lit name board for the stage.

Learns from signature-interaction sites such as Lando Norris by OFF+BRAND, Awwwards Site of the Year 2025.
